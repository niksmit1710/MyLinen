OUR STORY

Woven by Nature,
Tailored for Life

At MyLinen, we believe that the clothes we wear should be as enduring as the memories we make in them. Founded on the principles of sustainability and timeless design, we create pieces that celebrate the raw, honest beauty of flax.

Conscious Craftsmanship

Every MyLinen garment begins in the flax fields. We partner with small-scale weavers who treat every thread with respect. By choosing linen, we choose a path that consumes less water and leaves a smaller footprint on our Earth.

Our designs don't chase seasons. We create silhouettes that remain relevant decade after decade, softening and growing more beautiful with every wash.

Why Linen?

01

Breathability

Linen is a natural insulator. It keeps you cool in the heat of summer and retains warmth during cooler evenings, making it the perfect year-round companion.

02

Endurance

bruised by time. Linen is one of the world’s strongest natural fibers. Unlike synthetic blends, it won't pill or lose its shape, lasting for generations.

03

Hypoallergenic

Gentle on sensitive skin and naturally antibacterial. Because the flax plant requires fewer pesticides, the fabric remains pure and kind to your body.

04

Sustainable

Flax is a resilient crop that grows in poor soil with minimal water. Every part of the plant is used, ensuring zero waste from field to fabric.
